# Signal Reef screenshots

Capture real gameplay screenshots after installing a current build.

Use `tool/capture_store_screenshots.ps1` as a starting point, then manually review every image before Play Console upload. Do not upload old Arrow Puzzle screenshots, mockups, or unavailable gameplay features.

Recommended phone portrait set:

1. Active gameplay with HUD, player, enemies, and shots visible.
2. Gameplay moment showing a bounced signal shot.
3. Later wave pressure with a Pulse Seed and red projectile visible.
4. Home screen with Play, 20 waves, best score, and best wave.
5. Result screen after a real run.
6. Settings or help screen showing controls and feedback toggles.

Do not fake scores, results, enemies, upgrades, bosses, ships, ads, shops, leaderboards, reviews, awards, downloads, or unavailable modes.
