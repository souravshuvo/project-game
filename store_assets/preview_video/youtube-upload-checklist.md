# YouTube Upload Checklist

Use this after the final MP4 is rendered. Do not upload anything in this task.

## Upload Settings

- Upload as a standard YouTube video, not a YouTube Short and not a live video.
- Use a video URL for the Play preview video field, not a playlist or channel URL.
- Do not include URL parameters such as timecodes.
- Set visibility to public or unlisted for the Play preview video field.
- If also submitting through Google Play's YouTube Videos page, prefer public.
- Disable monetization and make sure no ads run against the video.
- Use only owned or properly licensed music, sound effects, fonts, and visuals.
- Avoid copyrighted music that can create monetization claims even if you turn ads off.
- Make the video embeddable.
- Do not make the video age restricted.
- Use a localized video for each localized store listing when practical.

## Title And Description

Suggested YouTube title:

Dots and Boxes Preview

Suggested YouTube description:

Preview gameplay for Dots and Boxes, an offline local board game with two-player
matches, bot practice, scoring boxes, extra turns, and quick rematches.

## Thumbnail

- Use the existing feature graphic or a still from real gameplay.
- Do not use fake awards, ranking badges, review stars, download counts, sale
  claims, or Google Play badges.

## Pre-Upload QA

- Final video uses only real app gameplay for UI shots.
- No fake board state, score, bot move, animation, or result appears.
- No ads appear.
- Captions are burned in and readable.
- Audio is licensed, clean, and not clipped.
- The first 10 seconds clearly show the actual app/game experience.
- The video is short and polished; target 24 seconds, stay under 30 seconds if possible.
- The uploaded video is recent enough for game promotion eligibility checks when needed.
