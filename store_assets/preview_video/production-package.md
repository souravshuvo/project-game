# Preview Video Production Package

## Concept

Close the Box.

A quick, readable gameplay video that shows the core loop: draw lines, take
turns, close boxes, keep scoring turns, practice against the bot, and finish
with a clear result.

## Delivery Mode

Production package only.

Final video rendering is not included because this task does not approve app
launch, emulator/device capture, install, or screen recording. Every UI shot
below must be captured from the real current app before editing.

## Story Rules

- Open with actual gameplay within the first 2 seconds.
- Keep at least 80 percent of the final video as real in-app gameplay footage.
- Use app UI only; do not show hands, device frames, fake taps, fake boards, or
  generated product screens.
- Keep captions short enough to read on muted autoplay.
- Do not include ads, rankings, reviews, awards, download claims, or calls to
  action such as "install now" or "play now."

## Timeline

## Real UI Capture List

1. Fresh 4x4 local match, empty board, Player 1 active.
2. First legal line draw.
3. Midgame local two-player board with both player colors visible.
4. Box capture moment.
5. Extra-turn moment after capture where the same player remains active.
6. Player vs Bot match in progress with bot difficulty visible.
7. Late board with multiple claimed boxes.
8. Final result screen showing winner or draw.

## Capture Safety

- Capture from the current app build only.
- Disable or avoid recording any post-match ad surface.
- Do not edit the board, score, turn indicator, or result values after capture.
- Do not use generated UI, mockups, screenshots from another app, or old builds.
- Avoid notification bars, debug banners, dev overlays, mouse cursor, emulator
  controls, or device chrome.
- If a capture state cannot be produced from real gameplay, cut that shot
  rather than faking it.

## Visual Style

- Board remains the visual hero.
- Background stays simple and light, matching the app surface.
- Use small, high-contrast captions in the lower safe area.
- Captions must not cover dots, active lines, scores, turn text, or buttons.
- Use gentle cuts and small focus highlights only; avoid slow decorative
  animation that delays gameplay.

## Audio Direction

- Music: light, family-friendly, no copyrighted track unless properly licensed.
- Mix: music under -18 LUFS integrated target, UI sounds clearly audible but soft.
- Sound effects: simple tap, valid line, capture reward, result chime.
- Voiceover: optional; if used, keep it short and natural.
- Captions should make the video understandable when muted.

## Render Specifications

- Duration: 24 seconds target, maximum 30 seconds preferred.
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 portrait.
- Resolution: 1080 x 1920.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS.
- Format: MP4.
- Video codec: H.264.
- Audio codec: AAC.
- Audio: present, licensed, and not clipped.
- Subtitles: burned in.
- Black bars: none.

## Supporting Assets

- Use `store_assets/feature_graphic/dots-and-boxes-feature-graphic.png` as the
  YouTube thumbnail candidate or closing style reference.
- Use `store_assets/app_icon/dots-and-boxes-play-store-icon-512.png` only on the
  closing title frame if the frame still feels clean.
- Do not use third-party game art, copied icons, stock gameplay, or fake store
  badges.

## QA Checklist

- [ ] Final video uses only real app footage for gameplay shots.
- [ ] First real gameplay appears within the first 2 seconds.
- [ ] At least 80 percent of the video represents actual app/game experience.
- [ ] No fake scores, fake boards, fake bot moves, fake results, or edited move outcomes.
- [ ] No ads appear in the video.
- [ ] No unavailable feature appears in footage, caption, VO, or title frame.
- [ ] Captions are readable on phone screens and do not cover gameplay UI.
- [ ] Audio is licensed, not clipped, and understandable with captions.
- [ ] No black bars, stretching, blur, sideways frames, debug UI, or cursor.
- [ ] Final YouTube upload is not monetized, is embeddable, and is not age restricted.
