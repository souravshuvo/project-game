# Human Footage Assets

No human footage is planned for this preview video package.

Reason:

- The app is a mobile board game.
- Google Play preview assets should prioritize actual app/game experience.
- Showing hands, device frames, or people interacting with a phone is unnecessary
  for this game and can distract from the board state.

If a future campaign needs human lifestyle footage, keep it separate from the
Google Play preview video and do not composite fake app UI onto devices.
