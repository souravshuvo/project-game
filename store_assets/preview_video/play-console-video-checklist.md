# Play Console Preview Video Checklist

Use this after the video is uploaded to YouTube. Do not publish anything in this
task.

## Where It Goes

- Play Console path: Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing.
- Section: Graphics.
- Field: Preview video.
- Input: one YouTube video URL.

## URL Rules

- Use the direct video URL.
- Do not use a playlist URL.
- Do not use a channel URL.
- Do not add extra parameters such as timecodes.
- Confirm the video is public or unlisted for the preview video field.
- Confirm the video is embeddable.
- Confirm the video is not age restricted.
- Confirm monetization is off and ads are not shown.

## Content Rules

- Actual gameplay appears early, ideally within the first 2 seconds.
- The first 10 seconds focus on real in-game experience.
- At least 80 percent of the video represents the real user experience.
- No unsupported features: online play, tournaments, leaderboards, shop, login,
  cloud sync, persistent progress, hints, undo, rewards, or live events.
- No post-match ads, YouTube ads, ad UI, or monetization messaging.
- No fake awards, rankings, reviews, ratings, downloads, or performance claims.
- No install/download/play-now call to action.
- No device frames, hands, or finger footage unless future gameplay becomes
  off-device, which this game is not.

## Final Asset QA

- Video has no black bars, stretching, blur, sideways frames, or cropped board UI.
- Captions are readable and stay off the board, HUD, and result buttons.
- Music and effects are licensed and family-friendly.
- The feature graphic still works as the video cover image in the Play listing.
- Screenshots remain accurate after the video appears before them on the listing.
- Re-run Prompt S6 before upload if app UI, screenshots, icon, or feature graphic changed.
