# Preview Video YouTube And Play Checklist

Do not upload anything during S5B. Use this checklist only after a final video has been created from real gameplay captures.

## YouTube Upload Checklist

- [ ] Upload a direct YouTube video, not a playlist or channel.
- [ ] Set video visibility to public or unlisted, not private.
- [ ] Turn ads/monetization off for the video.
- [ ] Confirm the video is not age restricted.
- [ ] Confirm embedding is allowed.
- [ ] Use a clean URL without timecodes or extra URL parameters.
- [ ] Use only original or properly licensed music and sound.
- [ ] Add captions or upload `captions.srt`.
- [ ] Use a thumbnail/cover consistent with `store_assets/feature_graphic/feature-graphic.png`.

## Play Console Checklist

- [ ] Paste only the direct YouTube URL into the preview video field.
- [ ] Confirm the first 10 seconds show actual app/gameplay experience.
- [ ] Confirm at least 80% of the video is representative of real gameplay.
- [ ] Confirm no ads appear in the video.
- [ ] Confirm no unavailable features appear.
- [ ] Confirm no install CTA appears.
- [ ] Confirm no ranking, award, review, price, sale, or download claim appears.
- [ ] Confirm no hands, device frames, notifications, or private data appear.
- [ ] Confirm portrait video has no black bars.
- [ ] Confirm captions are readable when muted.
- [ ] Confirm the feature graphic still works as the Play cover image.

## Final QA Checklist

- [ ] Video uses only real Tik Tak Toe app footage.
- [ ] Board marks, scores, results, and history rows were not edited in.
- [ ] The app name shown is `Tik Tak Toe`.
- [ ] Package used for capture is `com.childhood.tiktaktoe`.
- [ ] The video does not show old Arrow Puzzle assets.
- [ ] The video does not show online multiplayer, leaderboard, shop, skins, login, cloud sync, rewards, tournaments, or multiple AI difficulties.
- [ ] Audio is not too loud or distracting.
- [ ] Text is readable on a phone screen.
- [ ] Final length is 24-27 seconds.
