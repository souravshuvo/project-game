# Cloud Courier Climb Preview Video Package

Production package for a Google Play preview video. No final video has been uploaded or rendered from fake gameplay.

## Status

- Delivery mode: production package.
- Final MP4: not created in this pass.
- Reason: emulator/device recording was not approved, and S5B requires real app footage.
- Approved source evidence: real app-rendered screenshot assets in `assets/ui/`.

## Target Output

- Duration: 20-30 seconds.
- Orientation: portrait.
- Resolution: 1080x1920 preferred.
- Frame rate: 30 fps.
- Container: MP4.
- Video: H.264.
- Audio: AAC.
- YouTube use: upload as public or unlisted video for Play Console preview URL.

## Files

- `production-package.md`: full timeline, shot list, captions, voiceover, edit notes, capture steps, and QA.
- `shot_list.csv`: editor-friendly shot list with timings, source requirements, captions, and notes.
- `captions.srt`: timed caption file for the 24 second target edit.
- `assets/ui/`: real UI/gameplay source screenshots copied from the approved Play screenshot package.

## Truth Rules

- Use only real gameplay footage or real app-rendered screenshots.
- Do not show unavailable skins, shops, leaderboards, modes, rewards, maps, or fake rankings.
- Do not include ads in the preview video.
- Do not include fake awards, reviews, download counts, or performance claims.
- Re-capture real device/emulator footage before final video export if motion is required.
