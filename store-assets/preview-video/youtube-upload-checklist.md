# YouTube Upload Checklist For Google Play Preview Video

Use this after the final MP4 is rendered from real app footage.

## Upload Settings

- Upload as a normal YouTube video.
- Use one direct video URL in Play Console.
- Do not use a playlist URL, channel URL, or URL with extra timecode/query parameters.
- Privacy: public or unlisted.
- Embedding: enabled.
- Age restriction: off.
- Monetization: off.
- Ads: disabled. If any copyright claim causes ads to appear, replace the music/video and upload a clean version.
- Comments: optional; can be disabled to keep the listing tidy.

## Metadata

- Title: `Sixteen Breed - Sholo Guti / 16 Beads Preview`
- Description: `A short preview of Sixteen Breed, an offline Sholo Guti / 16 Beads board game with local play, bot practice, legal move highlights, captures, rematch, and match history.`
- Tags: `sixteen breed, sholo guti, 16 beads, board game, offline game`
- Thumbnail: use the real feature graphic or a real gameplay frame. Do not create fake gameplay.

## Play Console Entry

- Paste the single YouTube video URL into the Play Console preview video field.
- Confirm the URL opens the exact preview video, not a playlist or channel.
- Confirm the video can play when signed out.
- Confirm the video is not age restricted.
- Confirm ads are not shown before or during playback.
- Confirm the video is embeddable.

## Content QA

- First 10 seconds show actual app/gameplay.
- At least 80% of the video represents the real in-app or in-game experience.
- No black bars.
- No device frames unless they do not distract from gameplay.
- No fake UI, reviews, rankings, awards, downloads, or unsupported claims.
- No unavailable features: online play, tournaments, rewards, shop, account login, leaderboard, cloud sync, or chat.
- No visible test ads, interstitials, AdMob screens, analytics debug screens, notifications, emulator chrome, or cursor artifacts.
- Captions are readable while muted.
- Music and footage are owned or licensed for store use.

## Final File Target

- Filename: `sixteen-breed-play-preview.mp4`
- Duration: 20-30 seconds, target 28 seconds.
- Orientation: portrait.
- Resolution: 1080 x 1920 preferred.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS.
- Video codec: H.264.
- Audio codec: AAC.
