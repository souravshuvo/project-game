## Capture Rules

- Use real app UI only.
- Do not enable visible touch indicators unless a shot is unclear without them.
- Do not capture ads.
- Do not capture debug banners, notifications, emulator controls, cursor artifacts, logs, or broken states.
- Do not stage fake board states outside normal gameplay.
