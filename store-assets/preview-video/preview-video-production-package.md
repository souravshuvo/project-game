# Rooftop Rain Garden Preview Video Production Package

This package is for a 20-30 second Google Play preview video. It must be
assembled from real Rooftop Rain Garden gameplay footage only. Do not fake app
footage, do not show ads, and do not upload anything from this package until a
final video is captured, edited, and approved.

## Video Direction

- Orientation: portrait 9:16
- Target length: 28-30 seconds
- Style: calm, clean, readable, real gameplay first
- Audio: light upbeat instrumental, no dramatic trailer effects
- Transitions: simple cuts or short crossfades
- Text style: short overlay captions, high contrast, outside active controls
- Avoid: black bars, fake UI, ads, fake rewards, rankings, reviews, animals,
  towns, quests, social features, cloud sync, or unavailable modes

## Caption Text

Use `captions-en.srt` as the base caption file. Captions are intentionally
short so they remain readable on phones.

## Exact Capture Steps

1. Use the current release-candidate app build after build/run approval.
2. Hide debug banners and development overlays.
3. Start from a fresh local save for the opening, planting, and watering shots.
4. Capture a real Sun Sprouts plant action on an empty unlocked plot.
5. Capture a real watered crop with timer/progress visible.
6. Wait for a real ready crop or use an approved saved state that is achievable
   in the shipping game.
7. Capture Harvest, Sell crate, Buy seeds, and Upgrade from real game state.
8. Do not capture interstitials, banners, test ad labels, or ad loading states
   for this preview.
9. Export clean portrait footage without black bars.
10. Keep all edits faithful to the captured app UI.

## YouTube Upload Checklist

- Upload the final preview video to YouTube.
- Use a direct video URL, not a playlist or channel URL.
- Make the video public or unlisted.
- Disable ads on the YouTube video where possible.
- Do not age-restrict the video.
- Do not use copyrighted music or third-party footage.
- Keep title and description simple and consistent with the store listing.
- Verify playback on mobile before adding the URL to Play Console.

## Play Console Video Checklist

- The first 30 seconds show the real app experience.
- No unavailable features appear.
- No fake rankings, awards, reviews, downloads, earnings, or reward claims
  appear.
- No ads appear in the preview video.
- No black bars or stretched footage.
- Captions and overlays are readable on phone screens.
- Video matches the screenshots, icon, feature graphic, and store description.

## Final QA Checklist

- Real gameplay footage only.
- Main loop is visible: plant, water, grow, harvest, sell, upgrade.
- UI is not blurred, clipped, or blocked by overlay text.
- Text is short and readable.
- Audio is licensed or original.
- No personal device notifications or identifiers appear.
- Final URL opens to the intended video.
