# Privacy Policy

Dots and Boxes is an offline local game.

This version does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does
not include ads, analytics SDKs, account sign-in, cloud sync, online multiplayer,
location access, contacts access, camera access, microphone access, or push
notifications.

Gameplay happens on the device. Scores and match state are not sent off the
device.

If ads, analytics, online play, accounts, or cloud features are added in a later
version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated
before release.
