# Privacy Policy

Dots and Boxes is an offline local game. Gameplay does not require account
sign-in, cloud sync, online multiplayer, chat, location access, contacts access,
camera access, microphone access, or push notifications.

This version includes Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics SDK integration for
production testing and release measurement. Ads are limited to post-match
session breaks and are not shown during active gameplay. Analytics events are
used to understand app sessions, match starts, match finishes, grid size, mode,
bot difficulty, move counts, boxes completed, extra turns, invalid moves, and ad
load/show outcomes.

The app does not intentionally send player names, messages, contacts, precise
location, photos, audio, or user-generated content. Match history shown in the
app is stored only in memory for the current session.

Before Play Store release, the Google Play Data safety form must disclose the
AdMob and Firebase Analytics SDKs, including device/app identifiers, app
interactions, diagnostics, and advertising/analytics use as applicable to the
final configured SDK behavior.
