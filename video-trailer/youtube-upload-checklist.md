# YouTube Upload Checklist For Google Play Preview Video

## Before Upload

- Final video uses real app footage only.
- Final video is portrait 9:16 with no black bars.
- Final video avoids ads, emulator controls, notification clutter, and debug overlays.
- Music and sound effects are licensed for commercial store usage.
- Captions are burned into the video or available as checked caption files.
- No unavailable features are shown or mentioned.
- No "download now", awards, rankings, reviews, or download-count claims.

## YouTube Settings

- Upload as a normal YouTube video, not a Short-only upload.
- Visibility: public or unlisted.
- Do not set to private.
- Do not age-restrict the video.
- Disable monetization on the video.
- Ensure the video is embeddable.
- Use a direct video URL for Play Console, not a playlist or channel URL.
- Do not include timecode parameters in the Play Console URL.

## Suggested YouTube Metadata

- Title: `Emoji Chor Police Preview`
- Description: `A short preview of real Emoji Chor Police gameplay: secret roles, Police reveal, accusation, round result, and local progress.`
- Thumbnail: use the approved feature graphic or a real gameplay frame.

## Final Verification

- Open the uploaded URL in a signed-out browser.
- Confirm it plays without age gates or privacy errors.
- Confirm no YouTube ad appears before the preview.
- Confirm the video can be embedded.
- Confirm the Play Console field receives the clean YouTube video URL.
