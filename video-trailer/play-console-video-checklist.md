# Play Console Preview Video Checklist

## Listing Match

- App title: `Emoji Chor Police: Solo Game`
- Package: `com.childhood.emojichorpolice`
- Category: Games, Casual
- Video shows the current app experience.
- Video does not show pass-and-play, online multiplayer, chat, login, shop, tournaments, fake rewards, or cloud sync.

## Required Video Behavior

- Use a YouTube video URL.
- Do not use a playlist URL.
- Do not use a channel URL.
- Do not use a private video.
- Do not use a monetized video.
- Do not use an age-restricted video.
- Do not use copyrighted music or content without rights.

## Creative QA

- First 10 seconds show real app UI.
- Secret role privacy is clear.
- Police reveal is understandable.
- Accusation screen is real and not staged with fake UI.
- Round reveal/result is real.
- Progress or match summary is real.
- Captions are readable on mobile.
- No text overlaps important gameplay UI.
- No misleading claims or fake metrics.

## Upload Readiness

- Final MP4 has been reviewed locally.
- YouTube upload has been reviewed after processing.
- Feature graphic is uploaded or ready as the video cover context.
- Store screenshots do not conflict with the video.
- Data Safety and ads declarations match the app behavior.
