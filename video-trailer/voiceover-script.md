# Voiceover Script

## Recommended Voiceover

| Time | Text |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0-3.0 | Pick a short match and bot style. |
| 3.0-6.0 | Every round starts with hidden roles. |
| 6.0-10.0 | Reveal only your own card. |
| 10.0-14.0 | The Police must find the hidden Thief. |
| 14.0-18.0 | Choose carefully. One guess decides the round. |
| 18.0-23.0 | Scores update after every reveal. |
| 23.0-26.0 | Finish matches and track local goals. |

## Caption-Only Variant

Use this if natural voiceover is not available:

- Choose a quick match
- Secret roles are dealt
- Reveal only your card
- Police steps forward
- Pick one suspect
- Caught or escaped?
- Track local goals

## Recording Notes

- Keep voice calm, quick, and playful.
- Do not say "download now" or other calls to action.
- Do not say online, multiplayer, tournament, reward, shop, or login.
- Do not imply the viewer can choose the Police role; the role must be dealt by the real game.
- If using TTS, label it guide-only until a final human-quality voice is approved.
