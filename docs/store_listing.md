## Store Listing Copy

### Short Description

Steer marble crowds through gates and clear 30 quick arcade levels

### Full Description

Magnetic Marbles is a quick crowd-control arcade game about steering a stream of
marbles through simple gates, growing the group, and clearing compact enemy
clusters before the finish.

Hold and drag anywhere in the lane to guide the launcher. Pick readable gate
paths, build enough crowd strength, and collide with enemy blocks at the right
angle. Each level is short, handcrafted from original templates, and built for
fast retry.

Version 1 includes:

- 30 offline levels
- Simple hold-and-drag controls
- Add, multiply, drain, wide, and tight gate effects
- Enemy clusters, wide lines, and narrow blocks
- Level select, unlock progress, score, and stars
- Pause, restart, help, sound, and haptic settings

No login, leaderboard, shop, base-building, cloud sync, live events, or active
gameplay ads are included in version 1.
