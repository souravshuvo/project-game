### Full Description

```text
Signal Reef is an original arcade space shooter built around quick runs, drag movement, and one-bounce signal shots.

Guide a lone signal diver through corrupted star currents. Drag anywhere to dodge without covering the ship, auto-fire signal pulses, bounce shots off the arena walls, and clear compact enemy waves before your hull runs out.

Current features:

- Simple drag movement
- Automatic bouncing signal shots
- 20-wave offline survival run
- Drift Node and Pulse Seed enemy types
- Score and local best score
- Best wave progress saved on-device
- Hull-based survival
- Pause, retry, help, and settings screens
- Optional sound and haptic toggles
- No login required

Signal Reef is designed for quick arcade sessions, readable dodging, and satisfying score-chasing.
```

## Screenshot Storyboard

Use real captured app screens only. Phone portrait is the primary target because the current gameplay and drag controls are strongest in portrait.

Target export for phone screenshots:

- 9:16 portrait
- Minimum 1080 x 1920 recommended
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha
- Keep added tagline text under 20% of the image
- First three screenshots should prioritize real in-game UI

| Slot | Real screen or moment | Overlay copy | Alt text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Active gameplay with player, HUD, enemies, and shots visible | Drag to dodge | Player dodges enemies in a Signal Reef wave with score, hull, and wave HUD visible. |
| 2 | Gameplay showing a bounced player shot crossing the arena | Bounce signal shots | A signal shot bounces through enemies in the dark teal space arena. |

| 4 | Home screen showing title, Play button, 20 waves, and best progress | Quick offline runs | Signal Reef home screen with Play button, 20 waves, best score, and best wave progress. |
| 5 | Result screen after a real completed or lost run | Chase your best run | Result screen showing score, waves cleared, wave reached, best score, and Play again button. |
| 6 | Settings or help screen showing controls and feedback toggles | Clear controls | Settings screen explaining drag steering, auto-fire, hull, score, sound, and haptics. |

Do not show:

- Fake scores or fabricated results.
- Bosses, ship upgrades, shops, leaderboards, cloud save, live events, or rewarded ad features.
- Device frames, hands, unrelated art, fake ratings, or "download now" calls to action.
- Screenshots with debug banners, test ad clutter, notifications, or service provider details.
