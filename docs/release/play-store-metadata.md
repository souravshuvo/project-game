## Full Description

Tik Tak Toe is a quick tic tac toe game with a calm star-map style.

Play classic 3x3 X/O rounds on one device, choose a short match format, or face one balanced AI opponent built for casual games. Rounds are fast, readable, and easy to restart when you want one more match.

Features:

- Classic 3x3 tic tac toe
- Local two-player mode on one device
- One balanced AI opponent
- Single Round, Best of 3, and Best of 5 match formats
- Current score and recent match history saved on device
- Clear turn, win, draw, restart, and rematch feedback
- Sound and vibration settings
- No login, online multiplayer, shop, skins, cloud sync, or leaderboards

Tik Tak Toe is designed for simple same-device play, quiet polish, and short rounds that are easy to understand.
