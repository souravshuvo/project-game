## Full Description

Signal Reef is an original arcade space shooter built around quick runs, drag movement, and one-bounce pulse shots.

Guide a lone signal diver through corrupted star currents. Drag to dodge, auto-fire signal pulses, bounce shots off the arena walls, and clear compact enemy waves before your hull runs out.

Current features:

- Simple drag movement
- Automatic bouncing signal shots
- Short offline wave runs
- Drift Node and Pulse Seed enemy types
- Score and local best score
- Hull-based survival
- Pause, retry, and settings screens
- No login required

Signal Reef is designed for quick arcade sessions, readable dodging, and satisfying score-chasing.
