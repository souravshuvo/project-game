## Full Description Draft

Emoji Chor Police: Solo Game is a quick hidden-role score game inspired by childhood Chor-Police table play.

Reveal your secret card, wait for the Police, then see whether the Thief gets caught or escapes. Play against three bots on one phone, choose your match length, and track your local scores and challenge goals.

Features:

- One human player against three bots.
- Secret emoji roles: King, Minister, Police, and Thief.
- Quick, Classic, and Festival match presets with 5, 7, or 10 rounds.
- Bot styles that use only public scores or earlier revealed history.
- Local score history and challenge goals.
- No login, chat, online multiplayer, tournaments, or betting-like mechanics.
- Ads appear only outside active role reveal, accusation, and round reveal moments.

How a round works:

1. Reveal only your own secret role.
2. The Police is revealed.
3. Police accuses one hidden player.
4. All roles are revealed and scores update.
5. Continue to the next round or finish the match.

Scoring:

- King scores 1000 points.
- Minister scores 800 points.
- Police scores 500 points only if the Thief is caught.
- Thief scores 500 points only if the Police guesses wrong.

This is a small offline-friendly solo game for quick sessions and nostalgic score chasing.
