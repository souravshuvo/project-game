# KidsLand Preview Video Production Package - S5B

Status: production package only. No final video was rendered because the
repository does not contain real gameplay recordings, and emulator/device
recording was not approved for this pass.

## Delivery Mode

Production package.

This package is designed for a Google Play preview video made from real
KidsLand gameplay capture. Do not use generated gameplay, fake UI, fake
device frames, hands tapping a phone, third-party ad creatives, ratings,
awards, rankings, price language, sale language, or unsupported features.

## Official Play Requirements Used

Google Play supports one preview video by YouTube URL. The URL must be a
video URL, not a playlist or channel URL, and should not include extra
parameters. The video must be public or unlisted, not private, not
age-restricted, embeddable, and ads must be disabled. Google recommends games
show actual gameplay early, keep videos short, use the orientation that best
matches the app, avoid black bars, and use captions for muted playback.

Source: Google Play preview asset guidance.

## Recommended Render Specs

- Duration: 25 seconds.
- Orientation: portrait.
- Resolution: 1080 x 1920.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS.
- Format: MP4.
- Video codec: H.264.
- Audio codec: AAC.
- Captions: burned into video and uploaded as YouTube captions when practical.
- Audio: light royalty-cleared instrumental bed, gentle app SFX only if clean.

## Narrative Concept

Concept title: One Tap, Many Little Wins.

The video opens directly on gameplay, not a logo. It shows a quick sequence of
real activities a preschool child can understand visually: trace, count, sort,
match, remember, pop, draw, and celebrate. The emotional arc is simple:
curiosity becomes repeated small success.

## Real UI Capture Checklist

Capture only after the app can run cleanly.

1. Use a stable final or capture-approved build.
2. Disable ads for capture so no third-party ad creative appears.
3. Use a portrait phone or emulator approved for recording.
4. Hide debug banners, performance overlays, cursor artifacts, and system
   notifications.
5. Keep the status bar clean or crop it out consistently.
6. Capture active gameplay states, not empty menus.
7. Use the exact capture IDs above for file names.
8. Save raw captures in `docs/store-assets/preview-video/captures/raw/`.
9. Save trimmed clips in `docs/store-assets/preview-video/captures/trimmed/`.

## On-Screen Text Rules

- Use the short text in the shot list.
- Use large, high-contrast captions near the lower safe area.
- Do not cover gameplay controls, answer buttons, tracing paths, or feedback.
- Do not use "best", "#1", "top", "new", "free", "download now",
  "install now", "play now", ratings, awards, or review-style claims.
- Localize overlays before publishing in non-English listings.

## Voiceover Script

Practice numbers 1 to 10.
Tap the number you see.
Move shapes into baskets.
Find each shape's home.
Flip cards and remember.
Tap through quick waves.
Make simple drawings.

Voiceover is optional. If high-quality natural voice is not available, use
captions only.

## Audio Plan

- Music: gentle royalty-cleared instrumental loop, light percussion or plucks.
- Sound effects: only real app sounds if clean and not distracting.
- Mix: keep music below voiceover; avoid clipping.
- If child-directed policy review is incomplete, do not use recognizable
  commercial music or sounds.

## Edit Notes

- Keep the first 10 seconds dense: tracing, number tracing, counting, and
  sorting should appear immediately.
- Use clean cuts or very short pop transitions.
- Avoid long title cards, logos, or pre-rendered scenes.
- Keep at least 80 percent of the final video as real gameplay footage.
- End with real celebration footage, not a store badge or call to action.
